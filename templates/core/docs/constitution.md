# Engineering constitution

## Yorkie principles

1. Stay small: add only what earns its place.
2. Act big: verify with executable evidence.
3. Stay focused: keep sources of truth and tool choices few.
4. Guard the house: protect specs, security, secrets, and data integrity.
5. Bark early: make failures visible quickly.
6. Be fearless inside explicit boundaries.
7. Trust, but verify: deterministic checks beat judgment calls where possible.
8. Know your size: people own consequential decisions.

The completion gate is `./scripts/verify`, unless an environmental limitation
is recorded with the result.
